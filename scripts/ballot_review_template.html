How to read the numbers, sources and coverage

Adj net PPS is the model’s estimate of net points per set against its reference opponent. Adjusted hit gap is an attacking-efficiency differential, not the team’s actual hitting percentage. Performance is each match’s actual net PPS plus its opponent’s estimated strength. The median and middle 50% describe the consistency of those performances.

Per-game adjustments are retrospective descriptions using the season snapshot. Prediction tests use only earlier matches. Weights were learned from 2025 and frozen for 2026. On 2,921 later 2026 matches, AUC was .8041 for RPI + raw margins, .8249 with adjusted margins, and .8258 with the extra layers. The extra-layer improvement remains uncertain.

Availability and scouting notes are editorial context, not invented numerical injury or setting-quality adjustments. School sources verify results; linked reporting supplies availability and lineup context. A source may be team-focused and should not be treated as neutral scouting.

Known model gaps: Wisconsin–UCLA on November 1, Kansas–Utah on October 1, and one Texas A&M win. Corrected records and verified result context are shown separately; the fitted statistics are not silently changed. No causal altitude adjustment has been established. Missing values display as —. Match dates use Pacific time when a saved start time is available.
