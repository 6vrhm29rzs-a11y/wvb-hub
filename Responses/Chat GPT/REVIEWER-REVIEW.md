# Reviewer review — ChatGPT

**Useful foundation; needs a focused feasibility addendum.** The report makes the strongest distinctions between descriptive analysis, prediction, information timing, and unverified hypotheses. Its sensible direction is richer volleyball analysis plus controlled experiments, not immediately replacing POWER.

## Keep
- Separate the current baseline, the calibration population, and the hitting-scale floor into distinct experiments.
- Treat the previous component-model result as limited evidence, not proof that richer volleyball statistics can never help.
- Preserve observation timestamps, corrections, forecast cutoffs, and source lineage.
- Develop team/player dossiers even when a candidate statistic has not earned a predictive coefficient.
- Distinguish a passing average from its underlying distribution; identical averages can describe very different passing patterns.
- Investigate serve/receive and contact-value approaches only when the required events actually exist.

## Correct or qualify
1. **Other-project contamination:** references to Raven, an existing local-model setup, or a particular Mac mini are not evidence of an authorized WVB resource. WVB hardware, credentials, services, and permissions must be established independently. Do not reuse another project's private resources.
2. **Point sequences are not contact sequences.** Clean rally winners may support team serve/receive point-win rates. First-ball sideout, transition offense, set location, attack quality, and contact value require additional observations. Named-server availability also needs a coverage audit.
3. **Four-pass convergence is a question, not a demonstrated bug.** The affine/contraction argument needs the exact update code, initialization, normalization, missing-opponent treatment, and preseason blend. Display restandardization can change the argument.
4. **Keep the experiment menu small.** Seven plausible studies are not seven approved projects. First establish the exact baseline and evaluation contract, then a useful dossier, then one candidate with sufficient data.
5. **Technology availability and prices remain conditional.** The report's many model/API/CLI claims were not all independently checked. Require current primary evidence only for the few tools that survive prioritization.
6. **Statistical association is not causal attribution.** A relationship between passing, middle usage, and hitting does not isolate setter skill or prove injury effects. Role, opponent, rotation, and selection matter.
