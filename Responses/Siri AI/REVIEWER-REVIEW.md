# Reviewer review — Siri AI

**Useful exploratory leads, not yet an implementation specification.** The screenshot report was read visually; the clipped technology table was completed using the accompanying text.

## Findings
- A richer team/player analysis page is valuable now. It need not wait for detailed rally/contact data or for every statistic to improve a forecast.
- The claim that margin already contains all useful component information is too strong. Redundant information is a risk, but complementary or conditional information must be evaluated.
- The cited volleyball_analytics repository exists. Its README describes action recognition, game-state classification, and court segmentation. Reception-quality and advanced player KPIs are listed as future features—not demonstrated production-ready 0–3 passing or set-location outputs.
- Broadage and Data Sports Group have volleyball products. Their general marketing pages do not establish 2026 NCAA women's coverage, player-level fields, passing grades, historical availability, or affordable rights for this project.
- Julius is a real product; the quoted price and suitability for this exact workflow were not independently verified.
- A full-match video demonstration is too broad as a first feasibility gate. A small, manually labeled, representative rally sample could measure specific outputs, missingness, false attribution, and human correction time. Broadcast camera changes, replays, occlusion, identity, and rights need consideration.
- No authorized WVB hardware benchmark or permitted video dataset was established. The code license alone does not settle model-weight, training-data, or video rights.
