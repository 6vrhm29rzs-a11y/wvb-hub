## Measured facts (don't re-derive)

- Team per-set stats: `ncaa-api.henrygd.me/stats/volleyball-women/d1/{SEASON}/team/{id}` — **always pin the season; `/current/` is BANNED (see below)**. Paginated 7 pages × 50 = **343** teams (not 348 — see the reclassifier note below). **45 Hitting% (Kills/Errors/Total Attacks), 46 Kills/Set, 47 Assists/Set, 48 Aces/Set, 49 Blocks/Set (Solos+Assists sep), 50 Digs/Set, 51 W-L%.** RPI ranking endpoint = 348 teams but no raw RPI/SOS → derive + reconcile vs official rank ordering + figstats.
- **SOLVED 2026-08-09 — opponent points/set.** Not in `/boxscore`; it's in **`/game/{id}` → `linescores[]`** = `{period, home, visit}` per set (both teams ⇒ points for AND against, per set). `/boxscore` → `teamBoxscore[].teamStats.points` = the **match total** (rally points, NOT the kills+aces+blocks stat formula), and its per-set `sets[]` array is **attack-only** (kills/attackErrors/attackAttempts/hit%) — no scores. **Cross-check: Σ linescores == teamStats.points**, verified on 4 games (3-set, two 5-set incl. 15-13/15-12 deciders, championship 6500718). Game ids via `/scoreboard/volleyball-women/d1/{YYYY}/{MM}/{DD}/all-conf`. Dead endpoints: `gameInfo`/`scoringSummary`/`teamStats`/`linescore` → 422.
- **SOLVED 2026-08-10 — the game log IS enumerable.** `/scoreboard/volleyball-women/d1/{YYYY}/{MM}/{DD}/all-conf`, **no pagination**, one request per date. 139 dates (2025-08-15→12-31) ⇒ **5,131 unique games, season runs 2025-08-22 → 12-21**. `/game/{id}` returns per team `teamId`, `isHome`, **`division`** (the D-I flag RPI Factor I needs), `record`, `score` (sets), plus `linescores[]`. Crawler: `scripts/crawl_2025.py` (resumable, ~1.4 req/s, atomic checkpoints).
- **`/current/` IS BANNED IN THIS CODEBASE — always pin the season.** It resolves to whatever season ncaa.com considers active, so the same URL silently returns different data after a rollover: no error, just 2026 rows where 2025 was expected. **RESOLVED 2026-08-10:** the year segment *is* honored (`/2023/`→Nebraska 33-2, `/2024/`→Penn St. 35-2, `/2025/`→Nebraska 33-1), and a full 49-page regression confirmed **`/2025/` is byte-identical to what `/current/` returned** (`crawl_2025.py verify-pin`, 49/49). `SEASON` constant lives at the top of `crawl_2025.py`.
